Hissa 1

Accounting kya karti hai

Sabify me POS, purchases, expenses, payroll aur bank ka kaam pehle se hota hai. Accounting module in sab ko ek jagah — General Ledger — me double-entry ke qaide se likhta hai. Is se company ko asal Profit & Loss, Balance Sheet, Trial Balance aur Cash Flow milte hain.

Sab se zaroori baat: staff ko alag se ledger me kuch likhna nahi padta. Cashier POS pe sale karta hai, manager expense dalta hai, purchase wala Vendor Bill banata hai — system har 15 minute baad khud in sab ki ledger entries bana deta hai. Isay automatic posting kehte hain.

Ek sale ledger me kaise jati hai

Maan lijiye branch ne aaj 21,000 ki cash sale ki (18% sales tax ke saath), aur maal ki cost 12,500 thi. System raat tak is din ki ek entry banata hai:

JV-000231 · POS sales — Main Branch16-10-2026
AccountDebitCredit
1110 Cash in Hand21,000.00
4100 Sales17,796.61
2130 Output Tax (GST)3,203.39
5100 Cost of Goods Sold12,500.00
1140 Inventory12,500.00
Total33,500.0033,500.00

Har entry me Debit ka jod = Credit ka jod hota hai. Isi liye Trial Balance hamesha barabar aata hai.

Kaun kya kar sakta hai

KaamKaun
Kisi company ki accounting on / off karna, BackfillSuper Admin (Sabify team) — Operations › Accounting Setup
Accounting ke saare screens (Accounts Operations menu)Company ke Admin / Sub-Admin (package me shamil ho)
Sales tax return record karna, rate farq accept karna (PO Bill)Sirf Admin / Sub-Admin
Locked mahina unlock, band saal reopenSirf Super Admin
Vendor Bills, Exchange Rates, RFQJis role ko Purchase Order ki permission hai
Menu kab nazar aata hai

Accounting ke screens sirf us company ko sidebar me dikhte hain jis ki accounting on ki gayi ho. Sales Tax, Vendor Bills, PO Matching, Exchange Rates aur RFQ har company ko dikhte hain.

Hissa 2

Shuru karne se pehle tayyari

Accounting on karne se pehle company ke saath baith kar ye cheezein tay kar lein aur jama kar lein. Yahi aadha kaam hai.

Faisle jo pehle lene hain

SawalAam jawab
Company ka maali saal (fiscal year) kis mahine se shuru hota hai?Pakistan me aksar July (July–June). Ek dafa saal ban jaye to ye badal nahi sakta.
Accounting kis tareekh se shuru karni hai (start date)?Kisi mahine ki pehli tareekh, behtar hai maali saal ki pehli tareekh.
Purana data ledger me chahiye ya nahi?Agar purana data saaf hai to Backfill; warna start date se naya shuru + Opening Balances. (Hissa 3, qadam 3)
Kya company customer ledger "ulta" likhti hai (bill credit me)?Customer ka ek ledger dekh lein. Ulta ho to setup me ek checkbox lagana hai (qadam 7).

Start date se ek din pehle ke ye figures jama karein

  • Har branch ka cash (gin kar)
  • Har bank account ka balance — bank statement se
  • Stock — physical count, cost ke saath (stock count ka sheet)
  • Customers se lena hai — har customer ka udhaar (list)
  • Vendors ko dena hai — har vendor ka payable (list)
  • Fixed assets — furniture, machines, gaadi: khareed ki keemat, tareekh, ab tak ka depreciation
  • Koi loan, advance tax, salaries payable, owner ka capital
Yaad rakhein

System "Fill from system" se ye figures khud bhi bhar deta hai, lekin wo sirf andaza hota hai. Asal ginti aur bank statement se milana zaroori hai — ghalat opening = ghalat Balance Sheet.

Hissa 3

Setup — 8 qadam (ek dafa)

Ye qadam isi tarteeb se karein. Har qadam ke baad agle pe jayein.

1

Accounting on karein

Operations › Accounting SetupSuper Admin
  1. Company dropdown se company chunein.
  2. Fiscal Year ka start mahina chunein (aam taur pe July).
  3. Enable Accounting dabayein.

System khud bana deta hai: 43 accounts ka Chart of Accounts (Cash, Bank, Inventory, Sales, COGS, Expenses waghera), is saal ka fiscal year aur us ke 12 mahine. Ab company ke sidebar me Accounts Operations ke andar accounting ke screens aa jate hain.

Dobara dabana safe hai

Accounting on ho to button "Re-check setup" ban jata hai — kuch double nahi hota. Turn Off sirf menu chhupata hai; data delete nahi hota.

2

Chart of Accounts dekh lein

Accounts Operations › Chart of Accounts

Company ke accountant ke saath accounts ki list dekhein. Jo account chahiye wo bana lein, jo naam badalna ho badal lein.

  1. Naya account: + Account (top level) ya kisi group ke saamne + Sub.
  2. Code, naam aur parent group bharein, Save.
  3. Rent (6200), Utilities (6300), Bank Charges (6400) pehle se hain. Company ke apne kharche ke alag heads banayein — maslan 6600 Fuel, 6700 Marketing — taake P&L saaf aaye. Jo head na bane wo 6900 General Expenses me jata hai.

System wale accounts (Cash, Sales, Inventory, AP waghera) delete nahi hote, sirf un ka code / naam badal sakta hai — automatic posting inhi ko use karti hai.

3

Start date tay karein — purana data ya naya shuru

Operations › Accounting SetupSuper Admin

Raasta A — Naya shuru (zyada tar companies)

Purana data adhoora / ghalat ho ya sirf aage ka hisaab chahiye.

  1. Automatic Posting From = start date (maslan 01-07-2026) → Save.
  2. Qadam 4 me Opening Balances (start date se ek din pehle, 30-06-2026).

Raasta B — Purana data bhi (Backfill)

Company ka purana POS / purchase / expense data saaf hai aur pichle saal bhi chahiye.

  1. Post Older Data (Backfill) me Cut-off Date dalein (jis din se history chahiye).
  2. Preview — dekhein kitne saal / entries banengi aur kahan farq hai.
  3. Theek ho to Run Backfill. Purane fiscal years khud ban jate hain.
  4. Cut-off se ek din pehle ka hisaab Opening Balances me (qadam 4).
Dhyan

Automatic posting har 15 minute me sirf pichle 45 din dekhti hai. Agar 45 din se purana data badla jaye (maslan purani sale edit), to Backfill dobara chalana padta hai — dobara chalana safe hai, sirf farq post hota hai.

4

Opening Balances dalein

Accounts Operations › Opening Balances
  1. Opening Date = start date se ek din pehle.
  2. Fill from system dabayein — system cash, bank, stock, customers aur vendors ka andaza bhar deta hai, har line ke saath note ke yeh raqam kahan se aayi. (Is waqt kuch save nahi hota.)
  3. Hissa 2 wali asal figures se har line milayein aur theek karein. Fixed assets, loans, capital haath se dalein (Find Account se account dhoondein).
  4. Save & Post. Debit aur Credit ka jo farq bache wo khud Opening Balance Equity me chala jata hai.

Ghalti ho jaye to screen dobara khol kar theek karein aur phir Save & Post — purani opening entry khud reverse ho kar nayi lag jati hai.

5

Posting Rules — kaunsi cheez kis account me jaye

Accounts Operations › Posting Rules
  1. Expense Categories: har expense category ke saamne us ka expense account chunein (Rent → 6200 Rent). Jo khali chhodein wo General Expenses me jayegi.
  2. Bank Accounts: Sabify ke har bank account ke saamne Chart of Accounts ka bank account chunein (har bank ka alag GL account behtar hai).
  3. POS Payment Modes: Card / JazzCash / EasyPaisa / Online kis account me jayein. Default theek ho to rehne dein.
  4. Save Mappings, phir Post Now — pehli posting abhi chal jayegi.
  5. Neeche Needs Attention list dekhein — jo document post nahi hua us ki wajah wahan likhi hoti hai.
6

Products ki cost theek karein

Accounts Operations › Inventory Valuation

Profit tabhi sahi aata hai jab har bikne wale product ki cost maloom ho. System har product ki moving average cost khud nikalta hai (khareed, opening stock aur adjustment se).

  1. Show me Needs cost chunein — ye products bik rahe hain magar cost nahi.
  2. In ki cost Opening Stock / Vendor Bill / Stock Adjustment se dalwayein.
  3. Recalculate now — nayi average cost lag jati hai.

Jab tak cost nahi, us sale ki COGS 0 jati hai aur profit zyada dikhta hai.

7

Customer ledger ka sign (sirf zaroorat pe)

Operations › Accounting SetupSuper Admin

Kuch companies customer ke ledger me bill ko credit aur wasooli ko debit likhti hain (ulta). Agar aisa ho to Customer ledger: Credit increases balance tick karke Save karein, warna Accounts Receivable minus me aayega.

8

Accounting Health se check karein

Accounts Operations › Accounting Health

Ye page poore ledger ko check karke batata hai kya theek hai aur kya nahi. Har item ke saath likha hota hai kya karna hai, aur Open → se seedha us screen pe le jata hai.

  1. Laal (Fix first) items pehle theek karein — ye reports ko ghalat bana rahe hain.
  2. Phir peele (Check) items.
  3. Run checks again — sab hara ho jaye to company live hai.
Go-live ka matlab

Trial Balance barabar, Suspense ~0, cash minus nahi, ledger ka Inventory stock value ke qareeb, aur Financial Statements me Balance Sheet "Balanced". Pehle mahine ke aakhir pe accountant ke saath P&L aur Balance Sheet zaroor milayein.

Roz ka kaam

Jo system khud karta hai

Neeche wale kaam staff apni purani screens pe karta rahe — ledger entry har 15 minute me khud ban jati hai.

Sale returnSales Returns → Cash / Customer
Customer se wasooliCash / Bank → Accounts Receivable
Expense (Paid From cash ya bank)Expense account → Cash / Bank
Vendor Bill postInventory + Input Tax → Accounts Payable
Vendor ko paymentAccounts Payable → Cash / Bank
PO ka maal aaya (GRN)Inventory → Accounts Payable
Opening stockInventory → Opening Balance Equity
Stock adjustment (+ / −)Inventory ↔ Inventory Gain / Loss
Branch transferInventory (lene wali) ↔ Inventory (dene wali), Inter-branch Clearing ke zariye

Jo staff ko sahi tareeqe se karna hai

  • Khareed (purchase) hamesha Vendors and Purchases › Vendor Bills se karein — stock, vendor ka balance aur input tax ek saath sahi lagte hain. Sirf Stock Adjustment se maal dalne pe vendor ka payable aur tax ledger me nahi aata.
  • Expense dalte waqt Paid From (Cash ya Bank + account) zaroor chunein.
  • Vendor payment Vendor Bill ke Pay button se karein, taake payment bill se jud jaye.
  • Product cost: naya product ho to us ki cost bhi dalein.
  • Jo cheez kisi screen se nahi aati (loan, owner ka paisa, bank charges, kisi ghalti ki durusti) wo Journal Entries se manual entry.
Hissa 5

Har screen ka process

Har screen ke saath: kahan milegi, kab use karni hai, aur kaise.

Chart of Accounts

Accounts Operations › Chart of Accounts

Kab: naya kharche ka head, naya bank, naya loan account chahiye ho.

  1. + Sub us group ke saamne dabayein jis ke andar account chahiye (maslan Expenses ke andar).
  2. Code, naam, type bharein → Save.
  3. Jis account pe entries lag chuki hon wo delete nahi hota — Deactivate karein.

Fiscal Years

Accounts Operations › Fiscal Years

Kab: mahina band karna, naya saal banana, saal band karna.

  • Close (mahina) — us mahine me nayi entry nahi lagti. Reopen se khul jata hai.
  • Lock — pakka band; sirf Super Admin Unlock kar sakta hai. Audit ke baad lagayein.
  • + Next Fiscal Year — agla saal saal khatam hone se pehle bana lein, warna naye saal ki sales post nahi hongi.
  • Close Fiscal Year — saal ka profit / loss Retained Earnings me chala jata hai aur saare mahine band ho jate hain.

Posting Rules

Accounts Operations › Posting Rules

Kab: nayi expense category, naya bank account, ya koi document post nahi ho raha.

  1. Nayi category / bank ke saamne account chunein → Save Mappings.
  2. Post Now — 15 minute ka intezar kiye baghair posting chala dein.
  3. Needs Attention me jo error ho us ki wajah padh kar theek karein (maslan "No fiscal year" = agla saal banayein).
Mapping badalne ke baad

Nayi mapping sirf aage ki aur pichle 45 din ki entries pe lagti hai. Purani entries ke liye Super Admin se Backfill dobara chalwayein.

Journal Entries (manual entry)

Accounts Operations › Journal Entries

Kab: owner ne paisa dala / nikala, loan liya, bank charges, ya kisi account se doosre me durusti.

  1. + Journal Entry dabayein, Date aur Narration (kya hua) likhein.
  2. Har line pe account aur Debit ya Credit dalein. Neeche total — dono barabar hon to "Balanced".
  3. Save Draft (baad me) ya Save & Post (abhi ledger me). Draft ko baad me kholein to Post.

Ghalat entry: posted entry edit / delete nahi hoti. Entry kholein → Reverse → Reversal Date + Reason. System ulti entry bana deta hai; phir sahi entry dobara dalein.

General Ledger

Accounts Operations › General Ledger

Kab: kisi ek account ki poori tafseel dekhni ho (maslan Cash me kya aaya kya gaya).

  1. Account chunein (group chunein to us ke saare accounts), From / To date, branch.
  2. Opening, har entry, running balance aur closing nazar aata hai. Entry no pe click → poori entry.

Financial Statements

Accounts Operations › Financial Statements

Kab: mahine / saal ka nateeja dekhna, owner ya bank ko report deni.

  • Trial Balance — har account ka Debit / Credit; neeche dono barabar hone chahiye.
  • Income Statement (P&L) — Sales − Cost of Sales = Gross Profit − Expenses = Net Profit.
  • Balance Sheet — Assets = Liabilities + Equity. "Balanced" likha aana chahiye.
  • Cash Flow — cash kahan se aaya (Operating / Investing / Financing) aur kahan gaya.

From / To date aur Branch chunein. Kisi figure pe click se General Ledger khulta hai. Print se kaghaz pe.

Inventory Valuation

Accounts Operations › Inventory Valuation

Kab: stock ki value dekhni, ya profit ghalat lag raha ho.

  • Upar: stock ki kul value aur ledger ke Inventory se farq.
  • Show: Needs cost (cost nahi), Negative stock (minus stock), With cost.
  • Recalculate now — cost theek karne ke baad.

Vendor Bills

Vendors and Purchases › Vendor Bills

Kab: har khareed (bina PO). Ye stock badhata hai, vendor ka payable aur input tax lagata hai.

Naya bill

  1. + New Bill: Branch (maal yahan aayega), Vendor, vendor ka invoice no, Bill Date, Due Date.
  2. Add Item me product dhoondein; qty, rate, discount, tax %, (zaroorat ho to) batch / expiry.
  3. Other charges (freight waghera) — items ki cost me khud baant diya jata hai.
  4. Save Draft ya Save & Post. Post pe stock, vendor balance aur ledger teeno.

Bill ke baad

  • Pay — cash (branch) ya bank (account + cheque no). Thoda thoda bhi de sakte hain.
  • Return Goods (Debit Note) — maal vendor ko wapas: stock kam, payable kam.
  • Credit Note — vendor ne discount / rebate diya (maal wapas nahi).
  • Add Landed Cost — baad me aaya freight / duty / clearing; items ki cost badh jati hai, transporter ko payable.
  • Cancel Bill — sirf jab payment / note na ho aur maal bika na ho.
Approval

Agar company ne Approvals me Vendor Bill ka rule lagaya ho to post karne pe bill "Waiting for approval" me jata hai; approve hone pe khud post ho jata hai.

PO Matching (Purchase Order wali companies)

Vendors and Purchases › PO Matching

Kab: company Purchase Order + GRN use karti hai aur vendor ka invoice aaya hai. Is me PO, aaya hua maal (GRN) aur invoice teeno milaye jate hain.

  1. List me PO ke saamne Enter invoice. Draft khud ban jata hai — jitna maal aaya aur abhi bill nahi hua, PO ke rate pe.
  2. Vendor ka invoice no, date bharein. Invoice me jo qty / rate / tax hai wahi dalein. Kisi item ki qty 0 = wo agle bill me.
  3. Save & check — har item ka PO cost vs invoice cost aur farq % dikhta hai.
  4. Post. Rate 2% se zyada alag ho to Admin / Sub-Admin "Accept rate difference" tick kare.

Stock aur vendor balance GRN pe pehle hi lag chuke hote hain, is liye PO Bill sirf farq post karta hai (aur tax ko Input Tax me). Payment Vendor Bills se.

Exchange Rates (foreign currency khareed)

Vendors and Purchases › Exchange Rates
  1. Currency (USD, AED…), Date aur rate (1 USD = ? PKR) → Save rate. Rate jab badle naya dalein.
  2. Vendor Bill me Currency chunein — us din ka rate khud aa jata hai. Rates dollar me likhein; system PKR me save karta hai.
  3. Pay karte waqt amount dollar me + aaj ka rate. Bill aur payment ke rate ka farq 4960 Exchange Gain / Loss me khud jata hai.

Request for Quotation

Vendors and Purchases › Request for Quotation

Kab: kai vendors se rate le kar sab se sasta chunna ho.

  1. + New RFQ: title, branch, items + qty, aur vendors (Select vendors linked to these items se khud tick) → Create RFQ.
  2. Copy request text — WhatsApp / email pe vendors ko bhejein.
  3. Jawab aaye to har vendor ke saamne Enter quote: rate, discount %, tax %, delivery din. Na de to Declined.
  4. Comparison me har item pe sabse sasta hara; "Lowest full quote" wala vendor upar. Buy from me har item ka vendor chunein.
  5. Create Vendor Bills — har vendor ka draft bill ban jata hai; maal aane pe Vendor Bills se post.

Bank Reconciliation

Accounts Operations › Bank Reconciliation

Kab: har mahine — bank statement aur ledger ka bank account milana.

  1. Naya statement: bank account, mahina (from / to), statement ka opening aur closing balance.
  2. Import se bank ki CSV / Excel statement (Date + Amount, ya Debit / Credit columns), ya lines haath se.
  3. Auto-match — barabar raqam aur qareeb ki tareekh wali lines khud jud jati hain.
  4. Baqi: statement line pe click, ledger ki line(s) tick → Match. Bank charges / profit jaisi cheez jo ledger me nahi: us line pe Create entry.
  5. Farq 0 ho jaye to Mark Reconciled — statement lock ho jata hai.

Fixed Assets

Accounts Operations › Fixed Assets
  1. Naya asset: naam, asset account, cost, salvage (aakhir ki keemat), umar (mahine), tareeqa (straight line / declining %), kab se depreciation, pehle ka depreciation (purana asset ho to).
  2. Abhi khareeda hai to "paid from" chunein — khareed ki entry bhi ban jati hai.
  3. Depreciation har mahine 1 tareekh ko khud lagti hai; chahein to Run depreciation.
  4. Becha / khatam hua to Sell / Dispose: tareekh + kitne me bika — gain / loss khud 4950 me.

Cost Centers

Accounts Operations › Cost Centers

Kab: profit department / project / shop-wise dekhna ho.

  1. Centers tab: code + naam (SHOP, KITCHEN, MKT…).
  2. Rules tab: branch ka default center, aur expense category ka center → Save. Aage ki automatic entries pe khud lagta hai.
  3. Manual Journal Entry me har line pe cost center chun sakte hain.
  4. Report tab: har cost center ka P&L.

Budgets

Accounts Operations › Budgets
  1. Fiscal year, naam, (chahein to) branch / cost center → + New Budget.
  2. Edit Budget: har income / expense account ke 12 mahine. Saal ki raqam likh kar ↦ = 12 me barabar; ya Fill from last year's actuals + % (maslan +10).
  3. Save. Budget vs Actual tab me mahine chun kar farq dekhein — hara = budget se behtar, laal = bura.

Sales Tax

Accounts Operations › Sales Tax

Kab: har mahine ki sales tax return se pehle.

  • Monthly Summary — mahine wise sales (bina tax), FBR output tax, input tax (Vendor Bills se), FBR payable / refund, SRB. Not sent to FBR = FBR wali branch ki tax wali receipts jin pe FBR invoice number nahi — inhe zaroor dekhein.
  • Sales Register — receipt wise list, Download CSV.
  • Purchase Register — vendor bills, vendor ka NTN / STRN.

Return record karna

  1. Mahina khatam hone ke baad Summary me File FBR (ya File SRB).
  2. Jama ki gayi raqam, CPR / PSID no, tareekh. Accounting on ho to Post to ledger + Paid from account.
  3. Record Return. Ghalti ho to Returns Filed me Delete — ledger entry khud reverse.
Input tax

Input tax sirf Vendor Bills / PO Bills se aata hai. Jo khareed Stock Adjustment ya opening se dali gayi us ka tax claim yahan nazar nahi aayega.

QuickBooks Sync (sirf jo company QuickBooks use kare)

Accounts Operations › QuickBooks SyncAdmin / Sub-Admin
  1. Connect to QuickBooks → Intuit pe login → wapas Sabify me "Connected".
  2. Load QuickBooks accounts → Auto-match (code / naam se) → har "needs link" account ke saamne QuickBooks account → Save mapping.
  3. Send journal entries automatically tick + kis tareekh se → Save.
  4. Har 30 minute me khud jata hai; Sync now se abhi. Not sent list me wajah; theek karke Retry failed.
Hissa 6

Mahine aur saal ke aakhir ka kaam

Har mahine (agle mahine ke pehle hafte me)

  1. Accounting Health kholein, laal / peele items theek karein.
  2. Bank Reconciliation — har bank ka statement milayein.
  3. Fixed Assets — depreciation lagi hai ya nahi (1 tareekh ko khud lagti hai).
  4. Sales Tax — Not sent to FBR dekhein, return jama karke File FBR se record.
  5. Inventory Valuation — Needs cost wale products ki cost.
  6. Financial Statements — Trial Balance, P&L, Balance Sheet; owner / accountant ko dein.
  7. Fiscal Years — pichla mahina Close karein.

Saal ke aakhir

  1. Saal khatam hone se pehle + Next Fiscal Year se agla saal bana lein.
  2. Aakhri mahine ka upar wala saara kaam + stock count se Inventory milana.
  3. Accountant / auditor ki adjustments Journal Entries se.
  4. Close Fiscal Year — profit Retained Earnings me, saal band. Audit ke baad mahine Lock.

Hissa 8

Alfaaz ke maani

Debit / Credit
Har entry ke do pehlu. Asset aur kharcha debit se badhte hain; liability, capital aur aamdani credit se.
General Ledger
Saari entries ki kitaab, account wise.
Journal Entry (JV)
Ek lein-dein ki entry — kam az kam do lines, Debit = Credit.
Chart of Accounts
Company ke saare accounts ki list, groups me.
Fiscal year / Period
Maali saal (12 mahine) / us ka ek mahina.
Trial Balance
Har account ka balance; Debit aur Credit ka jod barabar hona chahiye.
Accounts Receivable (AR)
Customers se lena hai.
Accounts Payable (AP)
Vendors ko dena hai.
COGS
Cost of Goods Sold — bika hua maal kitne ka pada tha.
Moving average cost
Har khareed ke baad product ki nayi ausat cost; COGS isi se.
Input / Output Tax
Khareed pe diya gaya tax / sale pe liya gaya tax. Farq FBR ko.
Opening Balance Equity
Opening Balances ka farq yahan aata hai; accountant baad me Capital / Retained Earnings me le jata hai.
Suspense / Rounding (2190)
Jo raqam kisi account me fit na ho; isay ~0 rehna chahiye.
Adjustments Clearing (2195)
Manual adjustments jin ka doosra account pata nahi.
Retained Earnings
Pichle saalon ka jama profit; saal band karne pe P&L yahan jata hai.
Depreciation
Asset ki keemat ka har mahine kharche me jana.
Reconciliation
Do record milana — maslan bank statement aur ledger.
Backfill
Purane data ki ledger entries ek saath banana.
Reverse
Ghalat entry ki ulti entry — asal entry mitaye baghair durusti.
